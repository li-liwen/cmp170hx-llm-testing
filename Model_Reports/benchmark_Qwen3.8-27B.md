## Recommendations

- Use c=2 (interactive-optimal by TTFT/TPOT p99; per-user 35.7 tok/s) for interactive UX.
- Keep interactive pools at low concurrency rather than raising it solely for throughput, since latency-sensitive deployments should preserve the favorable TTFT and TPOT behavior seen at the optimal point.
- Treat higher-concurrency modes as an explicit batch/offline trade-off: route background jobs there if aggregate throughput matters more than per-user latency.
- Use p99 TTFT and TPOT as guardrails for autoscaling/load shedding; if adding concurrent interactive sessions moves p99 away from the c=2 profile, cap or shed load instead of continuing to increase concurrency.
- When scaling curves show diminishing throughput returns with rising tail latency, operate before that inflection for interactive serving and reserve post-inflection operation for batch.
- Separate long-context or long-generation requests from short interactive prompts when they are driving queue growth, because they can worsen TTFT/TPOT percentiles even under otherwise modest load.
- Favor separate scheduling paths for interactive and offline work, allowing offline tasks to utilize spare capacity while preserving the interactive latency envelope.
